# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

This policy explains how Diadal Nigeria Limited ("Diadal", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the `universal-social-auth` documentation website at `usa.diadal.com.ng` (the "Site") and the open-source npm package.

## The npm package does not collect data

`universal-social-auth` runs in your application's browser code and your own backend. It does not send data to Diadal, does not include analytics, and does not phone home. Tokens, client secrets, and user profiles stay in your application. You are the controller of any personal data your application processes.

## The Site

The Site is a static website served through Cloudflare Workers. It does not use cookies or analytics, and it does not require an account.

Like any web server, Cloudflare processes standard request data when you visit the Site. This includes your IP address, request time, requested URL, browser user agent, and referrer. Cloudflare handles that data under its own privacy policy. Diadal does not run server-side code that stores this data.

## Third-party links and services

The Site links to npm, GitHub, GitHub Sponsors, Patreon, and other services. When you follow a link or use an embedded badge, that service may receive your request and collect data under its own policy. Badges on the home page are images loaded from shields.io.

## Legal bases and your rights

Where data protection law applies, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Because the Site does not collect personal data through forms, accounts, or analytics, we normally hold very little. Contact us to make a request.

## Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the most recent change.
